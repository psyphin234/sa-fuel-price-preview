What moved it

Each month's change is set by the review period before it, here from Friday 30 January 2026 to Thursday 26 February 2026. Every day of it, CEF compares what fuel actually cost to import (the Basic Fuel Price) with the cost already built into the pump price. The period's average gap, the over- or under-recovery, is how much the price has to move to catch up.

Petrol 95 cost on average 21c a litre more to import than was built into the price (an under-recovery). Its import cost averaged R8.76 a litre. On its own that called for a change of +21c; the official change, +20c, was within a few cents of it.

Diesel (0.05% sulphur) cost on average 62c a litre more to import than was built into the price (an under-recovery). Its import cost averaged R9.92 a litre. On its own that called for a change of +62c; the official change, +62c, was within a few cents of it.

The rand averaged R16.00 to the US dollar over the period, stronger than the R16.31 of the period before (a stronger rand pulls fuel prices down).

Figures from CEF's daily Basic Fuel Price reports (cefgroup.co.za): prices from its pump, wholesale and maximum retail price rows, the averages from the last report of the period.
