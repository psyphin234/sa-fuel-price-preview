How this works, and what to trust

Every day already published by CEF (cefgroup.co.za) is read directly from their own daily report — that part matches CEF exactly. The one or two most recent days CEF hasn't published yet are estimated from US petrol (RBOB), diesel (heating oil) and Brent crude futures read at 15:00 London — just before the London Platts assessment CEF's figures are built from — plus the USD/ZAR rate. How much each market counts for each fuel is re-learned every hour from how CEF's own figures have tracked them over the past year; tested against 16 months of CEF history, this is typically within about 1.3% of CEF's real figure. It is still a proxy, not the real Mediterranean Platts assessment, so it can miss on days when European prices move differently from US markets. The predicted price move is the review-period average over/under recovery so far; the actual government announcement (effective the first Wednesday of the month) can differ because it also folds in separate slate-levy and fuel-levy decisions.

This data is generated on a private machine and pushed here on a schedule — it is not live. See the timestamp above for how fresh it is.
