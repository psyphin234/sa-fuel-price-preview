How this works, and what to trust

Every day already published by CEF (cefgroup.co.za) is read directly from their own daily report — that part matches CEF exactly. The one or two most recent days CEF hasn't published yet are estimated from free market benchmarks (ICE Brent, NYMEX RBOB gasoline, NY Harbor ULSD futures, USD/ZAR spot), scaled onto the last official Platts-based figure — a same-direction proxy, not the real Mediterranean Platts assessment. The predicted price move is the review-period average over/under recovery so far; the actual government announcement (effective the first Wednesday of the month) can differ because it also folds in separate slate-levy and fuel-levy decisions.

This data is generated on a private machine and pushed here on a schedule — it is not live. See the timestamp above for how fresh it is.
